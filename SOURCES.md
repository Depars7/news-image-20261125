## Lead

**Iran says two tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz.** Iran's
Revolutionary Guards Navy said two oil tankers struck naval mines, exploded
and caught fire early on Wednesday while using what Tehran called an
unauthorised route; the statement named no vessels and reported no
casualties. The claims are unverified. Washington's Virtual Embassy in Iran
told U.S. citizens to leave Iran immediately, warning hostilities could
escalate rapidly.
Sources: Reuters; IRNA via OilPrice; The Jerusalem Post; Daily Summary.

## Around the world

1. **United States — U.S. tells citizens to leave Iran.** The U.S. Virtual
   Embassy in Iran issued a security alert on Wednesday telling Americans
   to leave immediately, citing Iranian attacks on U.S. assets in Bahrain
   and Kuwait and the Houthi strike in Riyadh, and warning that Iran, the
   Houthis and other Tehran-aligned groups may target U.S. interests.
   Sources: Daily Summary; QNC News; The Daily Flare.
2. **Europe — Leak puts Russian war dead past 490,000.** BBC Russian's
   analysis of leaked Rosstat death records shows at least 490,334 Russian
   soldiers have died since the full-scale invasion began in 2022; experts
   put the true toll at 527,295–644,471, and Mediazona estimates it may
   have passed 700,000 with the missing counted.
   Sources: BBC Russian; CBS News / Bytes Europe; The Guardian.
3. **South Asia — Bangladesh raises garment wage 36%.** The
   government-appointed wage board approved a 36% rise in the garment
   minimum wage to 17,000 taka a month (from 12,500) in a 4–2 vote at
   about 2 a.m. Wednesday, after weeks of protests that shut factories.
   Sources: Reuters; Al Jazeera; The Daily Star.
4. **Pacific — Solomons reject foreign bases.** Solomon Islands and
   Australia signed a treaty in Canberra on Wednesday making Australia the
   Solomons' primary security partner; the treaty states there will be no
   foreign military or security base on Solomon Islands soil.
   Sources: Associated Press; CNN; The Wall Street Journal; Prime
   Minister of Australia (pm.gov.au).

## More from Wednesday

- **Americas — All hostages freed in Cartagena raid.** Colombian security
  forces stormed an Éxito supermarket in Cartagena early on Wednesday,
  ending a day-long siege that began after a failed armoured-vehicle
  robbery; all remaining hostages were rescued and one captor was killed.
  Sources: Reuters (AFP report); The Guardian; CNN.
- **Asia — Flooded Bogor gold mine traps miners.** Rescuers searched on
  Wednesday for possibly dozens of workers trapped 100 metres down in a
  flooded unauthorised gold mine in Pongkor, Bogor, West Java, after heavy
  rain flooded the shaft on Monday night; three more bodies were recovered
  on Wednesday, bringing the confirmed toll to six (AP; a local report
  later put it at seven).
  Sources: Reuters; Associated Press (via WCVB/KOCO).
- **Space — Pakistan's first astronaut reaches orbit.** China's
  Shenzhou-24 launched at 08:18 Beijing time on Wednesday and docked with
  the Tiangong space station at 11:56; Pakistani payload specialist
  Muhammad Zeeshan Ali is the first Pakistani in orbit and the first
  foreigner aboard Tiangong.
  Sources: Reuters; Xinhua; Aviation Week.
